# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps build a welcoming community:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, and assuming good faith.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning mistakes, apologising for them, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities of maintainers

Maintainers clarify these standards and take fair, proportionate action when behaviour falls short. They may edit, hide or remove
comments, commits, issues, pull requests and other contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they
judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful. They will explain the decision where it is appropriate to do so.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel run for the project) and when someone
is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems to the maintainers privately, using the contact options on the profile of the lead maintainer
([@knrl](https://github.com/knrl)). If you cannot do that, open an issue that says only "conduct report" and no details, and we will
arrange a private channel. Reports are handled in confidence, and reporters are protected from retaliation.
